watchduck // every tick computed by DuckDB, in your browser

A Swiss lever escapement built the “model the metal, not the maths” way: parts are polygons, DuckDB-WASM asks do these shapes overlap? (~79k ST_Intersects calls) to build the collision map, then a recursive SQL solver walks the map tick by tick. No physics engine — just SQL. Nothing in the code says “12° per beat”; that number falls out of the shapes.
